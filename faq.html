Frequently Asked Questions

What is TaskVexa?

TaskVexa is a task and promotion platform where workers can complete available tasks and promoters can create campaigns for workers.

What is the difference between Worker and Promoter?

Workers complete eligible tasks and submit proof. Promoters create and manage campaigns for workers.

How are task rewards handled?

TaskVexa rewards workers for successfully completing eligible tasks according to the reward amount displayed for each task.

Why is proof required?

Proof allows task submissions to be reviewed against the requirements supplied by the promoter or platform.

Are there activation fees?

Where an activation requirement applies, the current amount and conditions should be shown before activation. Users should review the applicable fees and terms before proceeding.

How do withdrawals work?

Withdrawal eligibility, minimums, processing rules, and any applicable fees are governed by the current platform rules. Check the withdrawal page and Terms of Use before requesting a withdrawal.

How can I get help?

Visit Customer Support for account, task, wallet, transaction, and platform questions.

Where can I verify official TaskVexa information?

Use the TaskVexa Verification page and only follow official links published by TaskVexa.
