How TaskVexa Works

TaskVexa has two account types: Worker and Promoter. Choose the account that matches what you want to do.

1Create your account

Register and select Worker or Promoter. Provide the information requested during registration.

2Workers access eligible tasks

Workers can view tasks available to their account, follow each task's requirements, and submit the requested proof.

3Proof is reviewed

Submitted work can be reviewed according to the task requirements. Approved work is recorded according to the platform's reward rules.

4Promoters create campaigns

Promoters can create tasks or campaigns for workers. Tasks may require platform review before they become available.

5Track your account

Use your dashboard to review task activity, wallet information, referrals, and withdrawal records where applicable.

Before you participate

Always read the task requirements and the current Terms of Use. Review any activation fees, withdrawal requirements, and other applicable charges before proceeding.
