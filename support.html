Customer Support

Need help with your TaskVexa account, tasks, wallet, transactions, referrals, or another platform feature? Start here.

Before contacting support

  • Use the email address associated with your TaskVexa account when possible.
  • Include your username and a clear description of the problem.
  • For a transaction issue, include the transaction reference and date. Never send your password or API keys.
  • For a task issue, include the task title and what happened.

Official support channel

Support contact: The official customer-support email, Telegram channel, or other support contact configured by the TaskVexa administrator should be used here.

Until an official contact is configured, please use the support contact displayed inside your TaskVexa account.

Common help

  • Account: Registration, login, password and account-type problems.
  • Tasks: Task availability, submissions, proof review and rewards.
  • Wallet: Balance, Airtime purchases and wallet transaction records.
  • Withdrawals: Withdrawal requests, status and payment details.
  • Referrals: Referral codes and referral activity.
  • Technical: Loading errors, failed requests and other platform issues.

Security reminder

TaskVexa support will never need your password. Do not share passwords, one-time verification codes, private API keys, or other sensitive credentials with anyone claiming to be support.
