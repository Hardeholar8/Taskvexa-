Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TaskVexa may collect, use, protect, and handle information when you use the platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information such as name, username, email, and account type.
  • Task submissions, proof, task activity, referral activity, wallet and transaction information.
  • Technical information needed for security, troubleshooting, and platform operation.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to create and secure accounts, operate tasks, process platform transactions, prevent abuse, provide support, and improve TaskVexa.

3. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate platform features, process requested services, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal requirements. TaskVexa does not sell personal information simply for advertising purposes.

4. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect account and transaction information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Responsibilities

Keep your password private and notify support if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain transaction records, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

TaskVexa or its service providers may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies to maintain sessions and improve functionality.

8. Children's Privacy

TaskVexa is not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use online services under applicable law.

9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the platform's practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact Customer Support

For privacy questions, account problems, wallet or transaction issues, contact TaskVexa through the official support contact provided inside the platform.
