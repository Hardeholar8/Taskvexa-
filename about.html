About TaskVexa

TaskVexa is a task and promotion platform designed to connect workers with available online tasks and give promoters a way to create and manage campaigns.

For Workers

Workers can browse eligible tasks, follow the requirements, submit the requested proof, and track task activity and rewards through their account.

For Promoters

Promoters can create campaigns for workers, submit tasks for review where required, and monitor campaign activity from their promoter account.

Our approach

TaskVexa is designed around clear task requirements, proof-based submissions, account activity records, and straightforward platform rules. Users should review the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, task requirements, fees, and withdrawal rules before using the platform.

Task rewards

TaskVexa rewards workers for successfully completing eligible tasks according to the reward amount displayed for each task.

For account or platform questions, visit Customer Support.
